End users opt in by visiting the TurboLimo booking page on our website and entering their mobile phone number in the booking form. Directly below the phone number field, users must manually check an unchecked consent checkbox before submitting the form.

The exact text displayed next to the checkbox is:

"I agree to receive booking-related text messages from TurboLimo, including confirmations, reminders, and ride updates. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

The checkbox is not pre-selected. Users must check it to proceed. By submitting the booking form with the checkbox selected, users provide explicit consent to receive transactional SMS messages related to their bookings. Privacy Policy and Terms links appear directly below the consent language on the same form.
